# Evaluation of the external BOT renewal-mechanics report

October 13, 2026. The submitted report is useful as a lead list, but its claimed statewide resolution of GPA mechanics is not established. We integrated **21 exact passages and five newly preserved originals** after checking the underlying documents. The explorer now has **179 document records, 177 preserved originals and 284 exact findings**. All 263 prior candidates and ten prior review judgments are retained unchanged; none of the additions is human accepted. Two append-only excerpt corrections improve highlight reliability without replacing original candidates; current review states are 272 pending and twelve corrected.

## Findings that improve the research

**TCU has the strongest contemporaneous linkage.** The 2009–10 catalog's BOT renewal paragraph explicitly requires a TCU cumulative GPA. Its registrar section defines the cumulative calculation using TCU work, excludes ordinary transfer credit, and uses the last TCU letter grade for repeats. These are same-catalog definitions, substantially stronger than assigning today's policy to a historical program. There is also a qualification the submitted report missed: qualifying study-abroad grades can enter the official TCU GPA through an advance election. These passages establish a published GPA definition, not staff batch code or transfer-appeal practice.

**UNT's distinction is supported, but historical repeat treatment remains unresolved.** The existing BOT page tells students with a low UNT GPA and qualifying overall transfer GPA to contact the office. Newly preserved current advising pages define those labels, but have no verified historical effective date. The retained 2013–14 catalog provides a historical resident-CGPA definition and says repeated work remains in the calculation until duplication is posted. It also distinguishes highest-grade departmental treatment from last-grade graduation treatment. That is a concrete reason not to backdate today's highest-grade advising rule to every BOT year.

**TAMIU's institutional-only SAP GPA is explicit in both retained policy versions.** We added the relevant passages from the 2019 policy and the earlier archived policy, whose printed effective dates conflict. The current HTML summary refers instead to attempted and transferred hours. Preserve this discrepancy; we cannot determine whether it reflects an error, a revision, or a difference in scope. The 2019 policy does not prove how first BOT renewals were handled years earlier.

**THECB explicitly corrected the 150-hour wording.** The new original notice rejects the wording that eligibility ends after 150 attempted hours and substitutes a limit on receiving BOT loans for more than 150 semester hours. It does not itself establish every detail of hour counting, nor explain the cause of the drafting error. The [official correspondence index](https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/correspondence/departmental-correspondence/?startRow=421) labels the notice November 14, 2018; the PDF itself is undated. We keep that provenance distinction and do not manufacture a dated eligibility-decision event.

**HelmNet separates certification, funding availability and disbursement timing.** The retained August 2020 reporting manual states that the Loan Approval Disclosure is withheld until funds are available and that the pending disbursement date is at least 11 business days after acceptance. This is a documented system constraint at that date, not proof that every borrower traversed a universal sequence or that the same rule applied throughout 2010–2020.

## Reproducibility assessment

Only the Markdown report was supplied. Its referenced CSVs, JSON package, query ledger and retained-source directory were not supplied and are not treated as verified artifacts. All its hyperlinks are wrapped in Google search URLs; we decoded the intended primary URLs, fetched originals, and created direct evidence links. Several claimed baseline identifiers do not match the actual explorer corpus. Its listed hashes generally describe extraction text rather than original PDFs, so they cannot establish original-file identity here. The report's 68-query and 22-new-document counts have not been independently reproduced.

This integration evaluates and incorporates the useful, source-supported contributions; it is not a fresh exhaustive search of every lead mentioned by the report. The most consequential remaining unknown is the campus-by-year GPA field and calculation actually used at renewal, including transfer appeals, repeat handling, rounding and the grade snapshot date.

## Verification

All 284 findings pass strict exact-anchor verification. The 263 prior candidate records, ten prior judgments and 174 prior document records remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Original PDFs match their registered hashes; public exports exclude private reviewer information and local paths. Two layout-sensitive excerpts use narrower exact selections while retaining their complete source context and original candidates. All 21 new highlights and all 16 public workflow checks pass in the staged read-only application, with no highlight-check page errors. Deployment results are recorded with the release.
