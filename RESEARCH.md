## Interpretation

There is evidence of campus review windows and general returning-student award schedules, alongside direct BOT processing order. Those pieces support a richer qualitative comparison. They do not create a balanced panel of BOT-specific GPA snapshots and award runs. A general SAP 2.0 review, BOT 2.5 eligibility, appeal decision, loan disclosure, and account credit are different events. Captured undated pages remain separate document versions under Unknown AY, including in the timeline.

Original SB4 establishes term-based loss/restoration language; later guidelines describe academic-year-end SAP. The recovered 2014 rule already uses year-end checkpoints and a separate unfunded-coursework restoration route, while grandfathering earlier cohorts. This does not establish when the year-end language first entered force or reconcile every earlier statutory version. The phaseout statute permits renewal terms before fall 2020, not including fall 2020. Neither legal inception nor phaseout alone identifies a campus's first or last actual disbursement.

## Final targeted routes

- **UT Austin's BOT borrower instructions, updated June 19, 2007:** the student first receives and accepts the BOT offer on E-FAN, then receives email directing them to complete the online application and promissory note. The source explicitly says this process repeats each year the student borrows BOT. This fills an annual loan-paperwork gap; it does not establish a separate application before college selection. [Offer/acceptance before application](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=utaustin-2007-bot-efan-email), [annual repetition](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=utaustin-2007-bot-yearly-process).
- **UIW's state non-renewal appeal form, captured May 2008:** a decision within 10 days, excluding incomplete appeals. The related state policy names BOT, while this form does not list individual programs. A separate general SAP form captured May 2010 describes Cardinal Mail warnings after spring grades post and a 10-day appeal review. [State appeal](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=uiw-2008-state-appeal-ten-days), [spring warning](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=uiw-2010-sap-spring-notice).
- **Southwestern Adventist:** archived general aid instructions place mailed/portal awards after documents are received and verified; 2014 policy and the 2019–20 catalog expressly review general SAP after fall, spring and summer. These do not separately date BOT's 2.5 review. [Award sequence](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=swau-2013-award-after-verification), [semester review](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=swau-2014-sap-term-review).
- **Lee:** a three-page excerpt from the college's 2008–09 catalog URL was recovered through a 2024 capture after the restricted historical-date archive query returned no captures. It repeats annual general state/federal SAP review and gives general aid priorities, but no review term or BOT award date. The catalog-year attribution comes from the original college URL, not the capture date. [Annual review](https://clibassi.github.io/texas-b-on-time-evidence-explorer/?evidence=lee-2008-sap-annual-review).

An empty date-bounded archive query therefore cannot establish that an old source is unavailable: a later capture can preserve the original historical document. The remaining precise BOT GPA freeze/ordinary award dates stay unknown where the inspected sources do not state them.
