## Remaining limits and access dispositions

- Exact Texas Tech 2014 strategy and SWAU 2017–18 financial-information originals remain unrecovered. Neighboring versions do not replace the missing editions.
- The UTRGV 2015–17 catalog path returns a “Page Not Found” body with HTTP 200; archive attempts returned an error and timeout. A UTPA predecessor catalog was recovered separately.
- Baylor archive responses were truncated and could not be validated as complete PDFs. They were not published as recovered originals.
- UIW’s linked BOT forgiveness replay returned 404; the external borrower-counseling route returned 429. They remain uninspected access limits, not evidence of absence.
- The Texas State Library finding aid identifies overlapping BOT-era physical records, including relevant board agenda/minute containers. No digital originals were identified for those folders; some access restrictions apply. No institution or archive was contacted.
- Official hosting for some training decks could not be recovered. Readable mirrors are labelled by their hosting and attribution limits, not treated as independent corroboration.
- Campus-specific GPA cutoff days, summer inclusion and ordinary renewal-award dates remain unknown where sources state only general SAP, request deadlines, disbursement windows or state allocation procedures.

This is a documented public-source search, not a complete institution-by-year census or proof that no further historical records exist. Archive capture dates, original document dates and website index dates remain distinct. For example, the FY2018 calculation memo is dated December 21, 2017 but was indexed January 5, 2018; UIW’s “1819” packet redirects to a copy revised April 2020.
